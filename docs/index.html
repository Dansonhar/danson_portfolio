AI Web AppReactGemini

Hunter — AI Job Hunter

An AI-powered job-hunting assistant that takes the grind out of finding work. Every morning at 9:00 a scheduled hunt pulls fresh roles from Google for Jobs (JSearch), screens out anything too senior or off-track, and has Google Gemini score how well each role fits my profile — with a requirement-by-requirement match analysis, level fit and a clear apply / skip recommendation. From there it tailors the resume and cover letter for each role, and tracks every application from discovered to offer. Built with React 19, Vite and Tailwind on the front end, an Express API on Vercel, and Supabase Postgres for profiles, jobs and applications.
